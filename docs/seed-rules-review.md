# Seed rules review checklist

These are the full set of migration seed values marked `VERIFY`. All remain open for accountable review. The sources below locate the material to review. The checklist does not approve timings or establish immigration requirements.

| Open   | `reporting_rules.worker_circumstances` v1, **10 working days**, effective 01/01/2026, no end date, `requires_verification=true` | Confirm supported events and scope, how the clock starts, exceptions, route-specific rules and the actual effective dates. Compare against the reporting duties in the current edition. [Home Office sponsor guidance, part 3](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance/workers-and-temporary-workers-guidance-for-sponsors-part-3-sponsor-duties-and-compliance-accessible#reporting-duties)             |

The two reporting seeds are in [the reporting migration](../supabase/migrations/20261122020000_reporting.sql), and the inactivity seed is in [the continuity migration](../supabase/migrations/20261122060000_personnel_continuity.sql).

- [ ] Capture reviewer, review date, source edition and paragraph, decision, effective period, supported event types, exceptions and staging evidence for each row. Record a named owner and next review date.
- [ ] Publish approved reporting values in new versions via a forward migration. Keep the previous versions and stored event-to-rule references intact. Set `requires_verification=false` only on reviewed versions and test event dates on both sides of the effective boundary. Add a new effective date to the inactivity configuration if the policy changes.
- [ ] Compare the England and Wales bank holidays for **2025–2028** with GOV.UK, including substitute and exceptional days. Verify enough published years to compute year-end deadlines and arrange future-year updates. These dates are a separate launch check. [GOV.UK bank holiday data](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json)
- [ ] Review the document expiry reminder intervals of 90, 30 and 7 days as product scheduling. Approve tenant retention separately. The app ships with no tenant retention periods set. [Documents](documents.md), [Privacy](privacy.md)
