# Incident response

Before launch, record the incident lead and deputy, deployment operator, security contact, privacy adviser, customer contact and vendor support routes in the restricted on-call directory. Keep an out-of-band contact route available.

1. **Declare and triage.** Open a restricted incident record with UTC timestamps, severity, observable symptoms, affected functionality, environment and who is coordinating. Treat suspected cross-tenant disclosure, privileged credentials exposed or unexpected ledger verification failures as urgent. Keep identifiers and confidential evidence out of shared chat, analytics and application logs.
2. **Contain.** Preserve the deployment and evidence before making changes where practical. Disable affected access or writes, revoke links and sessions where appropriate, rotate exposed credentials and redeploy consumers, and pause affected scheduled jobs. Treat already issued signed URLs as usable until expiry unless the object is removed or the provider invalidates the URL. Revoking ordinary app access alone may leave existing signed URLs usable. For billing or email issues, suspend affected deliveries and preserve provider references in the restricted record.
3. **Assess and communicate.** Record the scope, nature, discovery time and awareness time, the data categories, approximate counts and likely impact. Have the privacy lead identify processor and controller obligations for this incident. Where SponsorGuard acts as processor, notify the affected controller without undue delay and follow contractual requirements. For a notifiable UK GDPR breach where SponsorGuard is controller, notify the ICO without undue delay and within 72 hours of awareness where feasible. Assess whether affected people need direct notification and document decisions and reasons. [ICO personal data breach guide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/report-a-breach/personal-data-breach/personal-data-breaches-a-guide/)
4. **Recover.** Apply the reviewed fix or use the restore runbook. Verify tenant isolation, permissions, secrets, ledger integrity and any affected journeys before resuming traffic. Check whether provider deliveries or data changes outside the recovery point need reconciliation. Restore one worker or integration at a time and watch alert recovery. [Backup and restore](backup-restore.md)
5. **Close.** Capture the timeline, decisions, customer communications, root cause and follow-up owners. Rehearse changes to the runbook. Restrict evidence access, apply the agreed retention policy and record when evidence can be securely removed.

For status updates use confirmed facts, current impact, mitigations, the affected time window and the next update time. Use approved channels and have the incident lead approve public wording.
