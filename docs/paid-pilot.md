# Paid pilot and commercial test

SponsorGuard remains the product name pending a name, domain and trademark clearance decision. Docketwell is a working candidate. The visual identity uses aubergine, ink, warm paper and brass. Public headlines use Instrument Serif. Workspace and body text use Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.

## What the pilot sells

Start with one sector where the team can reach UK employers with roughly 10–50 sponsored workers and no in-house immigration team. Recruit five organisations willing to pay and designate a named HR lead. Choose one real weekly workflow for each participant, such as following up a check, gathering an expiry replacement or handing an item to an adviser. Use synthetic data for demos and early testing.

- Keep the existing Core price of £99/month for up to 20 workers and Pro price of £199/month for up to 60 workers during the pilot. The action queue and change review are Core workflows. Pro adds external evidence requests, alongside the existing Pro workflows. Adviser access follows existing consent and plan rules.
- Offer fixed-scope paid migration as an experiment. Agree the source format, record count, acceptance criteria and support hours in writing. Quote separately and record the cost of that work against the fee.
- Start with a single adviser partner already trusted by pilot employers. Ask them to work across two consented workspaces and hand an action back to each client. Record friction and the time needed to support the partner.

## What to measure

| Measure        | How to collect it                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Activation     | Time from onboarding to the first useful weekly queue, first assigned action and first file received where enabled.                                                                       |
| Customer value | Compare time spent gathering and transferring evidence before and during the pilot. Ask whether the queue replaced a recurring spreadsheet or email round.                                |
| Retention      | Count weekly use and paid renewals at the end of the pilot. Record reasons for renewal, downgrade and cancellation.                                                                       |
| Contribution   | Revenue excluding VAT less payment fees, attributable infrastructure, variable support and onboarding labour. Track acquisition costs, migration income and recurring revenue separately. |

At an illustrative £25 per staff hour, the monthly subscription corresponds to about four hours at Core and eight at Pro. Use each buyer's real cost and time saved when evaluating the purchase. A paid pilot is evidence of willingness to pay. Measure renewals and support costs before expanding the offer.

The product focuses on record collection and handover. Use the existing disclaimer in demos and avoid predictions about compliance outcomes. Before using real documents in the pilot, complete the launch gates and activate the approved malware scanner. [Launch checklist](launch-checklist.md)
